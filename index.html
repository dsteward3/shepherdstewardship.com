What we do

Three areas. One commitment.

01

Civil rights

When your rights are violated — by an institution, an employer, or the state — you deserve someone who will stand up and fight. We handle discrimination claims, due process violations, police misconduct, and more. No one should have to face injustice alone, and with Shepherd Stewardship, you won't.

02

Family law

Family legal matters are rarely just legal — they're deeply personal. Whether you're navigating a divorce, a custody dispute, an adoption, or a separation agreement, we bring both legal rigor and genuine compassion to every case. We help you protect what matters most while keeping conflict as low as possible.

03

Investment law

You trusted someone with your financial future. If that trust was broken through fraud, misrepresentation, unsuitable advice, or broker misconduct, you may have legal recourse. We represent investors in recovering losses and holding bad actors accountable — because your financial security deserves protection.

About the firm

Stewardship isn't just our name — it's how we work.

Shepherd Stewardship was founded on a straightforward belief: people facing legal challenges deserve more than a case number. They deserve an attorney who knows their name, understands their situation, and is genuinely invested in the outcome.

As a personal law firm, we work directly with every client from the first conversation to the final resolution. There are no hand-offs to junior associates, no lost context, no chasing someone down for an update. When you bring your case to us, you get focused, consistent, personal representation.

Our practice spans civil rights, family law, and investment law — three areas united by a common thread: people who need someone firmly in their corner. That is what Shepherd Stewardship is here to be.
